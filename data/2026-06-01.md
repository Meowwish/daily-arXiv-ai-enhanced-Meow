By combining the high-quality TPA pulsars and the accurate ionospheric subtraction of spinifex, we establish the most sensitive upper limits to the date on the ALDM Chern-Simons coupling, namely $\lesssim 10^{-14} - 3\times 10^{-13}\,{\rm GeV}^{-1}$, for the mass range of $[10^{-23},10^{-21}]\,{\rm eV}$ except at $m_a \sim 1.3 \times 10^{-22}\,$eV. This study underscores the great potential of constructing regular-pulsar PPAs for scientific tasks.
