### [44] [Two Point Correlation Function Estimation with Contaminated Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11283)
*Arya Farahi*

Main category: astro-ph.IM

TL;DR: 提出预测驱动的Landy-Szalay估计器（PP-LS），利用小规模光谱子集的精确标记和大规模目录的噪声标记，校正成像巡天中两点相关函数估计的偏差。

Motivation: 成像巡天中两点相关函数估计易受目标选择和数据处理中的污染与不完整性影响，导致真实与观测包含关系不匹配。这些误差常具有空间结构，会引入虚假大尺度功率或抑制真实聚类信号。高分辨率光谱样本虽能提供目标群体的精确包含标记，但通常只覆盖小部分天体。

Method: 提出预测驱动的Landy-Szalay估计器（PP-LS），结合全目录的噪声包含标记和小光谱子集的精确标记。通过基于残差的设计加权校正来去偏配对计数，仅需在标记子集上计算，无需概率校准、已知误分类率或污染显式建模。在简单随机采样的标记子集下，证明能恢复真实标记的Landy-Szalay配对计数。

Result: 在具有聚类和空间结构污染物的模拟中，PP-LS消除了朴素目录级估计器的偏差，同时实现了比仅用光谱数据聚类显著更低的方差。该估计器保持了标准随机目录归一化，可直接集成到标准配对计数流程中。

Conclusion: PP-LS估计器提供了统计原理严谨、计算轻量且可直接与标准配对计数流程集成的解决方案，能够在下一代巡天中实现稳健的聚类推断，有效利用小规模精确标记数据校正大规模噪声标记目录的偏差。

Abstract: The two-point correlation function (2PCF) is a cornerstone of precision cosmology, yet its estimation from imaging surveys is vulnerable to contamination and incompleteness arising from imperfect target selection and pipeline-level inclusion decisions. In practice, the scientific target is a physically defined population, while the working catalog is constructed from noisy measurements and selection cuts, leading to mismatches between true and observed inclusion. These errors are often spatially structured, correlating with survey depth, observing conditions, and foregrounds, and can imprint spurious large-scale power or suppress the true clustering signal. High-resolution spectroscopic samples provide gold-standard inclusion in the target population but are typically available for only a small subset of objects. We introduce a prediction-powered Landy--Szalay (PP--LS) estimator that combines noisy inclusion labels across the full catalog with exact labels on a small spectroscopic subset while preserving the standard random-catalog normalization for survey geometry and selection. PP--LS debiases pair counts using residual-based, design-weighted corrections computed only on the labeled subset, requiring no probability calibration, known misclassification rates, or explicit modeling of contamination. Under simple random sampling of the labeled subset, we establish recovery of the oracle (true-label) Landy--Szalay pair counts and thus consistency for the target 2PCF. In simulations with clustered and spatially structured contaminants, PP--LS removes the bias of naive catalog-level estimators while achieving substantially lower variance than spectroscopic-only clustering. The resulting estimator is statistically principled, computationally lightweight, and integrates directly with standard pair-counting pipelines, enabling robust clustering inference in next-generation surveys.
